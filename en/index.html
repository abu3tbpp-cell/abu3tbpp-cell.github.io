Who is Mohammed Nahed Bin Balous Al Otaibi?

Saudi content creator and occupational safety and health diploma graduate. Personal biography, education, family heritage and professional qualifications. He is currently employed and interested in Saudi heritage, horsemanship and tribal history.

Family and heritage

His father is Nahed Saleh Bin Balous. According to his family account, he belongs to the Al-Balous family of Al-Ghadabin, Otaibah. The account mentions Saleh, Balous and his brother Dofan, as well as Al-Nawami, Arja and Al-Tasrir. These family history details have not been independently verified.

Education and career

He studied at King Khalid Military College for three years before leaving without completing the program. He subsequently earned a diploma in Occupational Safety and Health from the Technical and Vocational Training Corporation, with a GPA of 4.02 out of 5 (Very Good), graduating on 22/12/1446 AH.

His personal motto: «النصيب ما يخيب».

Professional qualifications and training

  • Personal protective equipment, electrical safety, fall protection and lockout/tagout
  • Fire prevention and occupational safety specialist development
